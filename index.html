What makes "readers also liked" work?

Six million book ratings from 53,424 readers. For 10,000 of them, the most recent book they rated 4 or 5 stars was hidden, and each method had 10 tries to recommend it from what came before. The method mattered less than one choice: which of the reader's books to start from.

"Readers also liked" scores books by how often the same people liked them together. The taste profile model learns 64 hidden patterns from all ratings. The bestseller list ignores the reader.

Hits, series and variety

922 of the 10,000 hidden books (9%) are from the same series as the book the reader liked just before. Those are easy. The "other readers" column shows the rest.
